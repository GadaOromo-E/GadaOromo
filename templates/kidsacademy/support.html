Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

A parent or guardian sets up the account, then adds a child profile with a name (or nickname) and age. Your child can start learning right away.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email. We'll send you a password reset link. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or email us for help.

How is my child's learning progress tracked?

Kids Academy records lessons completed and progress so you and your child can see achievements over time. This data stays private to your account.

How do I change my child's name or age?

Open your account settings to update your child's profile information at any time. If you need assistance, contact our support team.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account from within the app's settings. When you do, your account and all associated data are removed. You can also email us and we'll help you complete the deletion.

Is Kids Academy safe for children?

Yes. Kids Academy is designed for children and complies with COPPA. We show no ads, do not track children, and never sell your data. Parents stay in control of the account at all times.
