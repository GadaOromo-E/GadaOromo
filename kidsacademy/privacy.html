Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Kids Academy is an educational app built for children. We take the privacy of every family who uses our app seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Kids Academy, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the app and support your child's learning.

Account Information

  • Parent or guardian email address
  • Child's name (or nickname)
  • Child's age

Usage Data

  • Lessons completed
  • Learning progress and achievements

Device Information

  • Device type
  • Operating system

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the service
  • Track and display your child's learning progress
  • Send password reset emails when requested

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Kids Academy is designed for children, and protecting them is our highest priority. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • Parents are in control. A parent or guardian sets up the account and controls all of the child's data.
  • No advertising or tracking. We do not show ads and do not use tracking technologies to profile children.
  • No selling data. We never sell or rent your data to third parties.
  • Minimal collection. We only collect what is necessary to provide the learning experience.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored securely using Google Firebase.
  • Information is transmitted over encrypted connections.
  • We apply reasonable safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access.

6. Your Rights

As a parent or guardian, you can at any time:

  • Access the data associated with your account
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Change or correct your information

7. Data Retention

We keep your information only for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is deleted.
